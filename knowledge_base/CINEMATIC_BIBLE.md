# CINEMATIC BIBLE

## Lens language

- 24mm: scale, environment, vulnerability, architecture
- 35mm: human context, movement, documentary intimacy
- 50mm: natural perspective, balanced hero coverage
- 85mm: portrait, luxury, compression, separation
- 135mm: observation, isolation, premium editorial detail

## Framing

Establishing shot sets geography. Medium shot carries action. Close-up carries thought. Extreme close-up isolates a decision. Low angle creates presence; high angle creates vulnerability; centered symmetry creates authority.

## Lighting

Key light establishes intention. Rim light separates the subject. Practical lights establish world. Volumetric haze is used to reveal depth, never as decoration. Blue hour supports reflection; hard noon supports discipline; warm side light supports legacy.

## Color grading

Keep skin natural. Protect highlights. Use teal and amber sparingly. Luxury favors blacks, champagne and silver. Futuristic scenes use deep blue and controlled cyan. Grain is subtle and consistent across an episode.

## Direction

Every camera movement must have motivation: reveal, approach, escape, observation or transformation.
